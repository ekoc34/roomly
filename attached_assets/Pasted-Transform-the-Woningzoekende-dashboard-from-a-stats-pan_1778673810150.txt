Transform the "Woningzoekende" dashboard from a stats panel into a dynamic "Search & Discovery Hub". The user should feel that their personal housing search journey continues here, not just see account data.

## TASK 1 — Add "Search Continuity" ("Ga verder met je zoekopdracht")
At the very top of the dashboard, show a prominent card that allows users to resume their last search instantly.
1.  Fetch the user's most recently viewed listing's location, or their last saved search filters, or their last used city filter from `listing_views` or `saved_searches`.
2.  Display a card with a title like "Ga verder met je zoekopdracht" and a summary (e.g., "Amsterdam • max €800 • Studio").
3.  A single click on this card must navigate back to `/kamers` with those filters pre-applied.

## TASK 2 — Replace "Snel navigeren" with "Aanbevolen voor jou"
1.  Remove the "Snel navigeren" section entirely.
2.  In its place, create a personalized "Aanbevolen voor jou" section.
3.  Fetch 4 listings based on the user's last viewed city, or the most recently boosted listings if no history exists.
4.  Display them in a grid, showing the listing image, title, price, location, and a "Nieuw" or "Uitgelicht" badge.

## TASK 3 — Turn "Mijn aanvragen" into a Timeline
1.  Transform the "Mijn aanvragen" list into a visual timeline.
2.  Each entry should show the application status as a step in a journey:

    -   "Bekeken door verhuurder" (if `read_at` is set on their message).
    -   "Reactie ontvangen" (if the status is 'accepted' or 'rejected').
3.  This reduces user anxiety by clearly showing where they are in the process.
