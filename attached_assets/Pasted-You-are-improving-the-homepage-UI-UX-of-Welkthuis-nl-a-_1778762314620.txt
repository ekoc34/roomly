You are improving the homepage UI/UX of Welkthuis.nl, a minimalist housing marketplace focused on trust, simplicity, and usability in the Netherlands.

Important context:
- The platform should feel calm, clean, modern, and trustworthy.
- Avoid feature overload, heavy animations, visual noise, or “startup SaaS” complexity.
- The homepage currently contains:
  - a hero section
  - two main CTAs
  - city chips
  - category chips
  - trust indicators
  - a search bar
  - listing cards
  - a “How it works” section
  - a large footer
- The current structure works, but the hierarchy feels too equal and visually crowded.
- The goal is refinement, not redesign.

Your tasks:

1. Refactor the hero section:
- Create a stronger visual hierarchy.
- Make the housing search flow the primary focus.
- Keep only ONE primary CTA:
  - “Zoek woningen”
- Keep ONE secondary CTA:
  - “Advertentie plaatsen”
- Make the search bar the central visual element.
- Reduce clutter above the fold.

2. Improve homepage copy:
- Rewrite the hero headline and supporting text so it feels:
  - trustworthy
  - human
  - calm
  - anti-spam
  - practical
- Avoid marketing-heavy language.

3. Simplify supporting UI:
- Reduce the visual dominance of:
  - city chips
  - category chips
  - trust badges
- They should support the search flow, not compete with it.

4. Improve listing cards:
- Keep cards minimal and highly scannable.
- Emphasize:
  - price
  - title
  - location
- Reduce visual weight of secondary metadata.
- Replace plain “Geen foto” text with a professional placeholder image or empty-image component.

5. Improve “How it works” section:
- Make it shorter and easier to scan.
- Use concise 3-step guidance.
- Avoid long explanatory text blocks.

6. Refine the footer:
- Keep footer minimal.
- Reduce unnecessary vertical space.
- Preserve trust and simplicity.

7. General design direction:
- Maintain current brand identity.
- Preserve mobile-first behavior.
- Improve spacing and hierarchy instead of adding more components.
- The UI should feel similar to:
  - Airbnb simplicity
  - Booking clarity
  - Funda practicality
- Do not introduce complex gradients, glassmorphism, or flashy modern effects.

Before implementing:
- Analyze the current component structure first.
- Then perform the improvements incrementally and carefully.
